Flexible Living Options

At Garden Plaza of Aurora, Colorado, it's all about options. Our independent living and assisted living community offer a variety of floor plans to meet your specific needs.

Each apartment is unfurnished, providing residents the opportunity to decorate and furnish the space with their own preferences, making it their home.

Bathrooms in each apartment are equipped with helpful devices to promote residents' safety and independence, including pull chains for emergencies. Associates are also available to assist when needed.

Independent living apartments feature a full kitchen, including a microwave and oven. Some models also include a balcony overlooking the campus.
